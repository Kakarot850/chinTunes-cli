## Stream / codec reference (verified 2026-11-24)

- JioSaavn CDN: `https://aac.saavncdn.com/<n>/<hash>_{96|160|320}.mp4` → `Content-Type: audio/mp4` (AAC-LC in MP4), `Accept-Ranges: bytes`, no auth/UA required. URLs are stable (not signed).
- YouTube (via youtube_explode): audio-only `mp4a` (AAC) or `opus` in WebM, both decoded by ExoPlayer. URLs signed + expiring. Data Mode itags: 249 (Opus ~50k), 250 (Opus ~70k), 251 (Opus ~160k), 139 (AAC ~48k) and 140 (AAC ~128k) as fallbacks. Video itags are never requested.
- YouTube Music InnerTube `POST music.youtube.com/youtubei/v1/next` (`WEB_REMIX`, unauthenticated): `RDAMVM<videoId>` gives about 50 tracks plus `nextRadioContinuationData`. Continuation pages give about 49 more with some overlap (dedupe needed). Verified 2026-11-24.
- LRCLIB: no key; send an identifying User-Agent. `/api/get` returns 404 when no exact match.
